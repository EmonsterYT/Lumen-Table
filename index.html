The room

A kitchen built for daylight.

Lumen Table is a neighborhood dining room on Solstice Avenue. We cook with yuzu, olive oil, and a wood fire, and we plate for color as much as flavor. Come for a long lunch, a weeknight table, or Sunday brunch that actually lets the morning in.
